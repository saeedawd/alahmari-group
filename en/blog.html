January 15, 2024 Al Ammar Al Saleem

Al Ammar Al Saleem Launches Major Construction Project in Riyadh

Al Ammar Al Saleem Contracting Company announced the start of a major construction project in Riyadh, featuring an integrated residential and commercial complex using the latest sustainable building technologies. The new project represents a qualitative leap in the construction sector in the Kingdom.

The project is distinguished by applying best environmental practices and sustainability, relying on eco-friendly building materials and modern energy-saving technologies. The project also includes integrated facilities and extensive green spaces aimed at providing a comfortable and sustainable living environment for residents.

This step comes within the framework of the company's strategy to expand in the Saudi market and provide advanced projects that meet customer needs and align with Saudi Vision 2030 in developing the real estate and construction sector.

December 5, 2023 Ohoud Ahmari

Ohoud Ahmari's Success in Providing Qualified Labor for the Private Sector

Ohoud Ahmari Company has achieved great success in providing qualified and trained labor for the Saudi private sector, contributing to supporting the local economy and enhancing the capabilities of national companies.
