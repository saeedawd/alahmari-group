# Al-Ahmari Group Corporate Website 

Official multi-language corporate web platform designed and developed for **Al-Ahmari Group** (مجموعة الأحمري), a leading multi-sector holding group based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia.

##  Overview

The platform showcases Al-Ahmari Group's corporate identity, core sectors, and subsidiary companies, supporting both Arabic & English languages (RTL & LTR) with a responsive, modern design aligned with Saudi Vision 2030 goals.

### Featured Subsidiaries & Sectors:
*  **Al Ammar Al Saleem Contracting** (Construction & Contracting)
*  **Ohoud Ahmari Contracting** (Labor Recruitment Solutions)
*  **Future House Real Estate** (Property Management & Real Estate)
*  **Sawaed Al Enjaz** (Business & Technical Solutions)

##  Key Features
* **Bilingual Support:** Full Arabic and English content switcher.
* **Corporate Profile & Portfolio:** Detailed service directories and interactive statistics.
* **Subsidiary Showcases:** Dedicated brand landing pages for each corporate branch.
* **Content & Articles:** Dynamic blog module for company updates and news.
* **Lead Generation & Contact:** Interactive contact forms and Google Maps integration.
